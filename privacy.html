What we collect

For this invitation-only service, we may collect your name, mobile number, SMS enrollment and opt-out records, messages you exchange with Hugh, your manually entered preferences, conversation summaries, and a small AI-maintained memory when that feature is enabled. We may also maintain operational logs needed for security and troubleshooting.

How we use information

We use this information to provide Hugh's Intellectual Stash, send authorized invitations and related account or participation messages, operate conversations, remember your stated preferences, personalize future responses, maintain service security, and honor enrollment or withdrawal requests. We do not sell participant information.

SMS consent and data sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent are not sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional use. Messaging providers and mobile carriers necessarily process the information needed to deliver and receive your texts; we use Twilio for that purpose. We may disclose information if legally required.

Private memory and access

Hugh's service may summarize conversations and maintain a small personalized memory to help make responses more relevant. Participants can request that their learned memory be reset. Application-level access controls are intended to prevent other participants and ordinary administrator dashboard users from viewing your personal memory. The operator of the self-hosted computer may have technical access to the underlying system and data. Please do not share information you would not want stored.

Storage and deletion

Conversation records and memory are maintained on the service's self-hosted systems. The operator can remove an account and its associated personalization records. Other records, operational logs, and backup copies may remain for operational or legal reasons until their applicable retention periods expire. The service is being developed; contact the person who invited you to request access, correction, or deletion.

Messaging and your choices

Messages concern invitations, enrollment, conversation participation, and account-related notices. Message frequency varies and can include daily participation invitations when the service is active. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving SMS or HELP for assistance. SMS consent is voluntary and separate from agreeing to participate in conversations.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate service channel.
