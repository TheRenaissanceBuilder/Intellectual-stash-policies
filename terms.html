The program

Hugh's Intellectual Stash is a private, invitation-only experience featuring AI-assisted conversations on science, philosophy, history, literature and related topics. Participation is voluntary, and AI-generated material may contain mistakes.

How SMS enrollment works

People who have separately agreed to receive an invitation may receive an automated SMS from Hugh's Intellectual Stash. An invited person may confirm participation by replying with the exact text YES 1701. This confirmation does not by itself provide access to another person's private account. SMS consent is not required to purchase any goods or services.

Messages, frequency and charges

Messages may include invitations, confirmations, conversation participation notices, and account-related information. Frequency varies; messages may occur daily when the service is active. Message and data rates may apply. Delivery is subject to mobile network availability.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to cancel SMS messages at any time. Reply HELP for assistance. You can also contact the person who invited you for support. After opting out, you may receive a final confirmation message. Do not reply YES 1701 if you do not want to enroll.

Privacy and personal memory

Hugh may save conversation context and a small personalized memory to make future conversations more helpful. Available reset and account controls allow participants to manage their own personalization. See our Privacy Policy for collection, use, storage, and sharing information.

Service availability

This small, independently operated service may be interrupted, modified, or discontinued. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
